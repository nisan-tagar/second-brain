## 🎤 INTERVIEW SKILLS

**Year Goal:** Build systematic interview preparation habits and gain real-world practice

### Technical Interview Prep

**Data Structures & Algorithms**

- [ ] Solve 100+ LeetCode problems by year-end (focus: medium 70%, hard 20%, easy 10%)
- [ ] Master core patterns: two pointers, sliding window, BFS/DFS, dynamic programming
- [ ] Weekly practice: 3-5 problems minimum (consistency over cramming)
- [ ] Track weak areas and iterate on problem types

**System Design**

- [ ] Study 20+ system design case studies (YouTube, blogs, books)
- [ ] Practice designing 10+ systems from scratch (whiteboard or Excalidraw)
- [ ] Master key concepts: scaling, caching, databases, message queues, CDNs
- [ ] Document 3-5 real systems you've built at work (portfolio pieces for interviews)

**AI/ML Specific**

- [ ] Prepare to discuss LLM architectures, fine-tuning, and deployment
- [ ] Be ready to explain your AI projects in depth (design decisions, trade-offs)
- [ ] Study AI system design: RAG pipelines, vector databases, prompt optimization
- [ ] Practice coding AI features live (e.g., "build a simple RAG system")

### Behavioral Interview Prep

**STAR Method Stories (Prepare 10+)**

- Leadership: Led team through ambiguous project, resolved conflict, mentored junior engineer
- Impact: Delivered project ahead of schedule, optimized system performance, drove adoption
- Failure: Project failed, missed deadline, technical debt decision—what you learned
- Collaboration: Cross-team coordination, stakeholder management, consensus building
- Innovation: Proposed new approach, experimented with new tech, improved processes

**Tech Lead Scenarios**

- How would you prioritize conflicting requests from PM, designer, and executives?
- Your team is behind schedule. What do you do?
- Two senior engineers disagree on technical approach. How do you resolve?
- How do you handle underperforming team member?
- Describe your approach to building team culture and psychological safety

### Mock Interview Practice

**Q1-Q2: Foundation**

- [ ] 2 mock technical interviews with peers (data structures/algorithms)

- [ ] Record yourself answering common questions; review and improve delivery

**Q3-Q4: Real Practice**

- [ ] 4+ real interview loops at companies (practice under real conditions)
- [ ] After each interview: document questions asked, review performance, identify gaps
- [ ] 2 additional mock interviews focusing on weak areas identified

## 🧭 GUIDING PRINCIPLES (2026 Edition)

**1. Foundation Over Flash** This is a building year. Establish systems, routines, and habits that compound. Don't chase shiny objects.

**2. Interview Readiness is Career Insurance** Consistent interview prep (LeetCode, system design, behavioral) keeps options open and confidence high. Treat it like working out—small, regular reps beat cramming.

**3. Document Everything** Write down your investment philosophy, learnings, decisions. Document your technical work and STAR stories. Future you will thank present you.

**4. Ship, Don't Perfect** Launch those AI applications and technical content. Focus on building your portfolio and reputation, not perfection.

**5. Network Intentionally** Every conversation with a growth-stage company is market research. Take notes, build relationships.

**6. Stay in Accumulation Mode** Reinvest everything. No lifestyle inflation. Let compounding do its work.

**7. Track to Improve** You can't optimize what you don't measure. Track portfolio, fitness, time, learning, and interview practice.
