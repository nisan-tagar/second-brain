**End State:** Tech Lead at pre-IPO company with $500K+ equity package

- Lead 2-3 high-visibility AI/ML projects at current company

- Build portfolio of production AI applications

**Year 2 (2027): Position for Transition**

- Push for promotion at current company
- Interview at 10+ growth companies; receive 3+ competitive offers

**Year 3 (2028): Make the Leap**

- Accept Tech Lead role at pre-IPO company (Series C/D minimum)
- Negotiate 0.1-0.3% equity ownership with 4-year vest
- Build team of 5-8 engineers within 6 months

**Year 4-5 (2029-2031): Scale Impact**

- Grow team to 15+ engineers
- Drive initiatives directly impacting revenue/growth
- Position for Director/VP track post-IPO
- Exercise equity strategically based on company trajectory

## 💰 FINANCE

**End State:** $1M investment portfolio + $5M total net worth

### Portfolio Strategy

**Growth Portfolio (60%)** — Target 12%+ CAGR

- 40% Mega-cap AI infrastructure (Amazon, Google, +1 TBD)
- 30% SMH semiconductor ETF
- 20% SCHG broad growth anchor
- 10% Small-cap moonshots (individually researched, 10x potential)

**Dividend Portfolio (40%)** — Tax-efficient compounding

- 65% Compounding machines (dividend growth + appreciation)
- 15% REITs (tax-deferred accounts only)
- 10% SCHD (dividend growth anchor)
- 10% IVLU (value diversification)

**Operating Principles:**

- Reinvest ALL dividends (accumulation phase)
- Semi-annual rebalancing via new capital (minimize tax drag)
- Individual stocks for conviction plays; ETFs for broad exposure
- Maintain thesis coherence without concentration risk

### Milestones

**Year 1 (2026): $300K portfolio**

- Contribute $30K + employer match + returns
- Research and deploy moonshot allocation (3-5 positions)
- Finalize third mega-cap AI position

**Year 2 (2027): $450K portfolio**

- Increase contributions to $40K/year
- Rotate moonshot positions (harvest gains, redeploy)
- Optimize tax location (REITs in tax-deferred)

**Year 3 (2028): $650K portfolio + vesting equity**

- Increase contributions to $50K/year (tech lead salary boost)
- Begin receiving vested equity ($50-100K annually)
- Develop equity exercise vs. portfolio deployment strategy

**Year 4-5 (2029-2031): $850K → $1M+ portfolio**

- Scale contributions to $60K+/year
- Equity appreciation: $200-500K (conservative IPO scenario)
- Real estate: $200-300K equity (investment property or upgrade)
- **Total net worth crosses $5M** (portfolio + equity + real estate)

## 🧭 GUIDING PRINCIPLES

**Compounding Everything**

- Small, consistent progress beats intensity
- Systems outlast motivation
- Compounding applies to skills, health, wealth, and relationships

**Thesis Coherence**

- Every goal should reinforce others
- Career enhances portfolio knowledge; side projects enhance career skills
- Health enables sustainable high performance

**Tax Efficiency First**

- Minimize tax drag on wealth accumulation
- Structure equity and investments optimally
- Think in after-tax returns, not gross numbers

**Optimize for Upside**

- Take asymmetric bets with capped downside
- Moonshot portfolio allocation, equity packages, side projects
- Position for 10x outcomes, not linear growth

**Stay Flexible**

- Revisit plan quarterly; pivot when data changes
- Markets, opportunities, and personal priorities evolve
- Discipline in execution, flexibility in strategy
