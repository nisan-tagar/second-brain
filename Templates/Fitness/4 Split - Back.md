## 🔹 Warmup (10–12 minutes)

| Component                  | Duration | Notes |
| -------------------------- | -------- | ----- |
| **Cardio (Running / Stationary Bike)** | 4–5 min | Moderate pace, elevate heart rate, break a light sweat |
| **Dynamic Stretching**      | 3–4 min | Full body: leg swings (front & side), arm circles, torso rotations, hip circles |
| **Band / Activation Work**  | 3 min   | Light resistance bands: <br>• Glute bridges or banded side steps (glutes/hips) <br>• Band pull-aparts or external rotations (shoulders) <br>• Monster walks or banded squats (legs/core stabilizers) |

### 🔹 Notes
- Perform **each movement with control**, focus on full range of motion.  
- Adjust intensity so you are **warm but not fatigued** at the end of the warmup.  
- Use this warmup before any push, pull, or legs session.
