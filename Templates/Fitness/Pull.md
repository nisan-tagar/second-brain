### 🔹 Notes
- Perform **each movement with control**, focus on full range of motion.  
- Adjust intensity so you are **warm but not fatigued** at the end of the warmup.  
- **Progressive Overload:** Once you can hit the top of the rep range for all sets, increase weight.  
- **Form > Ego:** Every rep should be clean, controlled, and intentional.  
- **Listen to Your Body:** If forearms/biceps are overly fatigued after main pulls, reduce isolation to 2 sets. Back work is priority.
