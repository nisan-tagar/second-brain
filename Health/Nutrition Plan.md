## Principles

- No snacking between meals
- No calories after 19:00
- Aim for 150-170 grams of protein daily
- On rest days, try to skip breakfast and add a protein shake after 11:00
- If working out early in the morning, pre-workout is optional instead of breakfast, and post-workout is a protein shake.
- Coffee window: 08:00 - 15:00
