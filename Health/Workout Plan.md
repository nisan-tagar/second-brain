### Goal: Train for hypertrophy (Muscle Growth)

* **Intensity**: 65–85% of 1RM  
* **Reps**: 6–15 reps  
* **Solid Volume:** With 20-22 total sets per session (high-volume routine) geared perfectly for muscle growth.
* **Sets per muscle group per week**: 10-20 sets  
* **Rest Period**: 60–90 seconds between sets
